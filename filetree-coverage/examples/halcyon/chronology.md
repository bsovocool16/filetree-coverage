**Gaps — what the room should contain and does not (3):**
1. *Board approval of Amendment No. 1 to the 2021 Stock Plan.* The amendment recites Board approval on May 1, 2023 and stockholder approval on May 10, 2023. No Board consent of May 1, 2023 is in the room. The stockholder consent of May 10, 2023 (3.05) approves the A&R Certificate and the Series A financing "on the terms presented" and does not mention the Plan; whether "the terms presented" included the Plan increase cannot be determined from the room.
2. *Approval of Amendment No. 2 to the 2021 Stock Plan.* The amendment recites no Board or stockholder approval, and no consent covering it is in the room. Plan §14 allows the Board to amend "subject to stockholder approval where required". The chronology records the amendment as the Plan's text presents it; its authorization is for the reviewer.
3. *Schedules and exhibits.* None of the financing agreements' schedules or exhibits are in the room (Schedule A Investors and Schedule B Key Holders to the IRA, ROFR and Voting Agreements and their restatements; Exhibit A Purchasers and the Disclosure Schedule to each SPA). Party lists and holdings therefore cannot be confirmed from the room, and the room copies of several agreements reproduce non-contiguous sections (for example the 2022 Meridian SAFE lacks §1(a); the Voting Agreement runs §1.2, §3, §7.8). Every citation above should be confirmed against a complete original.

**Other matters for the reviewer:**
- *SAFE conversion rests on the SPA alone.* Both 2022 SAFEs are recorded as converted and terminated at the Series A Initial Closing on the strength of Series A SPA §1.1 ("shall convert ... at the Initial Closing in accordance with their terms") and SAFE §5. No conversion notice, closing certificate or stock ledger is in the room. The subsequent appearance of Ridgecrest and Meridian as signatories to the Series A agreements is consistent with conversion but is not itself evidence of share issuance.
- *Certificate of Amendment (2022-06-30) filing date.* The instrument states its execution date (June 30, 2022) but not a filing date; that it was "filed June 30, 2022" is recited only in the A&R Certificate of Incorporation (1.03). Confirm against the Delaware filing record.
- *Signature blocks and consent thresholds.* The room copies of the Voting Agreement (3.04), Amendment No. 1 to it (3.06), the ROFR and Co-Sale Agreement (3.03) and the A&R ROFR (3.07) show signatures for the Company and investors only; no Key Holder signature appears, although Voting Agreement §7.8 and ROFR §6.8 require Key Holder majority consent to amend, and Amendment No. 1 to the Voting Agreement was signed by the Company, Cobalt and Ridgecrest only. Amendment No. 1 to the A&R IRA (4.06) is signed by the Company, Northwind and Cobalt; A&R IRA §6.6 requires 60% of Registrable Securities including Northwind, and Northwind signed, but the 60% cannot be verified without the schedules. The A&R IRA's recital that the signatories constitute the majority needed to amend the 2023 IRA is likewise unverifiable from the room. Validity is a question for the reviewer; the chronology reports what the documents show.
- *Instruments with no signature block.* The Bylaws (1.05), A&R Bylaws (1.06), 2021 Stock Plan (5.01) and Plan Amendments No. 1 and No. 2 (5.02, 5.03) carry no signature or secretary's certificate in the room; their adoption dates are taken from the "Adopted"/"Effective" statements in their own text. The scan reported these as "unknown" execution status; they are treated here as adopted per their text, subject to confirmation.
- *Board size across the operative stack.* The operative A&R Bylaws (2023-05-12) fix the Board at five (Art. III §2), while the operative A&R Voting Agreement (2025-02-20) provides for seven directors (§1.2) and the Second A&R Certificate caps the Board at seven (Art. IV §3.3(e)). No bylaw amendment after May 12, 2023 is in the room. Confirm whether one exists or whether the bylaws were left unamended at Series B.
- *Management Rights Letter (4.05)* is signed by the Company only in the room copy.
- *Series B Additional Closings.* The Series B SPA §1.1 contemplates up to 2,750,000 additional shares at Additional Closings within 90 days; nothing in the room shows whether any occurred.
- *Dates taken from file names:* none. The scan initially attributed the dates of the 2021 Bylaws, both SAFEs, the Side Letter and the Management Rights Letter to their file names; on opening, each date was found in the instrument's text (the Bylaws' "Adopted March 15, 2021"; the SAFEs' payment date "on or about July 8, 2022"; the two letters' "February 20, 2025" date lines). The draft Second A&R IRA's August 1, 2026 date is a draft-header date, not an effective date.
